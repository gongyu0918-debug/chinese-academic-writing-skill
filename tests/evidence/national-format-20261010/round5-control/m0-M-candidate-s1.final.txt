**Abstract**

This study plans to examine the experience of borrowing registration in community reading rooms. The author has organized 13 pieces of registration feedback, but their classification is not yet complete. Once permission is obtained, the author plans to interview 3 users and to describe the borrowing experience in combination with the registration feedback. The interviews have not yet been conducted, and the materials contain no measures of satisfaction or borrowing efficiency. A descriptive analysis is expected, without prejudging which type of problem is most common.

**Keywords**

community reading room; borrowing registration; user experience
