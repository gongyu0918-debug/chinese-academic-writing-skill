Abstract: This study proposes to examine the experience of borrowing registration in community reading rooms. The author has compiled 13 registration feedback records but has not yet completed their classification. After obtaining permission, the author plans to interview 3 users and to describe the borrowing experience in combination with the registration feedback. The interviews have not yet been conducted, and the materials did not measure satisfaction or borrowing efficiency. The study is expected to produce a descriptive analysis, without prejudging which type of problem is most common.

Keywords: community reading room; borrowing registration; user experience
