Abstract

This study examines the experience of borrowing registration at community reading rooms. Thirteen pieces of registration feedback have been collected and have not yet been sorted into categories. The plan is to interview three users once permission is obtained and, together with the registration feedback, to describe the borrowing experience. The interviews have not yet been carried out, and the material contains no measurement of satisfaction or borrowing efficiency. A descriptive analysis is expected; no judgment is made in advance about which type of problem is most common.

Keywords: community reading room; borrowing registration; user experience
