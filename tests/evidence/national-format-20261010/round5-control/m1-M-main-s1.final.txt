Abstract

This study intends to examine the borrowing registration experience in a community reading room. The author has organized 13 registration feedback records but has not yet classified them. After permission is obtained, the author plans to interview three users and use the registration feedback to describe the borrowing experience. The interviews have not yet been conducted, and the materials do not include measurements of satisfaction or borrowing efficiency. The study is expected to produce a descriptive analysis and will not pre-judge which type of problem is most common.

Keywords: community reading room; borrowing registration; user experience
