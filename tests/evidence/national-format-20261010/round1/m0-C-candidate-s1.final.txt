**Abstract**

This study plans to examine the user experience of campus printing services. Seven problem records have been compiled to date; interviews have not yet been conducted. The study intends to interview six users once permission is obtained and to organize the materials around equipment location, queuing experience, and operational feedback. Analytical categories will be determined after the records and interview materials are read. Service efficiency has not been measured so far, and differences among users have not been examined. The study expects to produce a descriptive analysis of the user experience.

**Keywords**: campus printing; user experience; interview plan
