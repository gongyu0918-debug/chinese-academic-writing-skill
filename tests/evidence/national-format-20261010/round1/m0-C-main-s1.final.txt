### Abstract

This study plans to examine the user experience of campus printing services. To date, 7 records of reported problems have been compiled; interviews have not yet been conducted. The plan is to interview 6 users after permission is obtained and to organize materials around device location, queuing experience, and operational feedback. The categories of analysis will be determined after reading the records and the interview materials. At present, service efficiency has not been measured and differences among users have not been examined. The expected outcome is a descriptive analysis of the user experience.

### Keywords

campus printing; user experience; interview plan
