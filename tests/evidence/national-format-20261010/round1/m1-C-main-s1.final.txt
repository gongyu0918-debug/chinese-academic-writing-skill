Abstract: This study aims to examine the user experience of the campus printing service. The author has compiled seven problem records; interviews have not yet been conducted. Once permission is obtained, the study plans to interview six users and organize the material around device location, queuing experience, and operational feedback; the analysis categories will be determined after the records and interview materials have been read. At this stage, the study does not measure service efficiency or examine differences among users. The study is expected to produce a descriptive analysis of the user experience.

Keywords: campus printing; user experience; interview plan
