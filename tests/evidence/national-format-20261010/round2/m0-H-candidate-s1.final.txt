**Abstract**

The proposed study examines the arrival experience of users after they reserve a seat in a library. Eleven feedback records have been collected but have not yet been categorized. Once permission is obtained, four users are to be interviewed, and their accounts will be combined with the feedback records to organize the arrival experience. Interviews have not been conducted, and neither the reservation success rate nor the arrival rate has been measured. A descriptive analysis is expected; for the time being, no conclusions are proposed on improving effectiveness.

**Keywords**

seat reservation; arrival experience; feedback records
