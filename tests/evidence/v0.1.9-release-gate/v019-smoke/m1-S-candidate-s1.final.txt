Abstract

This study intends to analyze the usage records of self-service borrowing and returning lockers. The author has saved 18 registration records, which have not yet been classified. After obtaining permission, the author plans to interview five users and, in combination with these records, conduct a descriptive analysis. The interviews have not yet been conducted; the materials do not include measurements of user satisfaction or queuing time, and no improvement effects are presumed.

Keywords

self-service borrowing and returning lockers; usage records; user experience
