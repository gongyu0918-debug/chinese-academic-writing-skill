Abstract
This study intends to analyze usage records of campus self-service borrowing and returning lockers. The author has saved 18 registration records, which have not yet been classified. Five users are planned to be interviewed after permission is obtained, and the records will be combined with the interview material for descriptive analysis. The interviews have not yet been conducted, and the material does not measure satisfaction or queuing time; no improvement effect is assumed.

Keywords: self-service borrowing and returning lockers; usage records; user experience
