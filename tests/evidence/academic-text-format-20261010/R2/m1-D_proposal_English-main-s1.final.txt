Abstract: This study intends to examine the use experience of community reading spaces. The author has compiled 12 venue introductions and plans to interview 8 adult users; the interviews have not yet been conducted. The research focuses on how users describe the convenience and limitations of the spaces, without presupposing that the spaces can increase reading frequency. The expected output is a thematic analysis of the use experience.

Keywords: Community Reading Space; Use Experience; Thematic Analysis
