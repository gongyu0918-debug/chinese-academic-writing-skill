Abstract

This study plans to examine the use experience of community reading spaces. The author has organized 12 site introductions and plans to interview 8 adult users; the interviews have not yet been conducted. The research focuses on how users describe the convenience and limitations of the spaces, and does not presuppose that the spaces can increase reading frequency. The expected output is a thematic analysis of the use experience.

Keywords: Community Reading Spaces; Use Experience; Thematic Analysis
