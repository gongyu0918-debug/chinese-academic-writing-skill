**Abstract**

This study examines how adult users describe their experiences of using community reading spaces. The study is at the proposal stage: twelve venue introductions have already been collected as background material, and interviews with eight adult users are planned but have not yet been carried out. The analysis attends to how users describe what is convenient and what is limiting in these spaces, and it does not presuppose that community reading spaces increase reading frequency. The expected outcome is a thematic analysis of the experiences that users report.

**Keywords**: Community Reading Spaces; Usage Experience; Thematic Analysis
