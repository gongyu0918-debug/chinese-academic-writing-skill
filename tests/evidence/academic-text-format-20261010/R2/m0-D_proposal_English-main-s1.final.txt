## Abstract

This study proposes to examine the usage experiences of community reading spaces. It asks how users describe the conveniences and constraints they encounter in such spaces, and it does not presuppose that these spaces raise the frequency of reading. Twelve venue introductions have already been compiled. Interviews with eight adult users are planned but have not yet been conducted. The expected outcome is a thematic analysis of these usage experiences.

## Keywords

Community Reading Space; Usage Experience; Thematic Analysis
