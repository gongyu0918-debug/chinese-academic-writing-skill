**英文题名（sentence case）**

The relationship between online course prompts and students' self-reported engagement

**Abstract**

This study analyzed 86 valid questionnaires completed by students in a single online course at one institution. The questionnaires recorded the students' ratings of the clarity of the course prompts and of their own learning engagement. The two ratings were positively correlated (r = 0.28). The study did not test whether the prompts caused changes in engagement, and its conclusions are limited to the questionnaire sample from this course.

**Keywords**

online courses, prompt clarity, self-reported engagement
