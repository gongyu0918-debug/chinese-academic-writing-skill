Title:
The relationship between online course prompts and students' self-reported engagement

Abstract:
This study analyzed 86 valid questionnaire responses collected in one online course at a certain institution. The questionnaires recorded students' ratings of the clarity of course prompts and of their own learning engagement. The results showed a positive correlation between the two ratings (r = 0.28). This study did not test whether the prompts caused changes in engagement, and the conclusions are limited to the questionnaire sample from this course.

Keywords: online course, prompt clarity, self-reported engagement
